About INIVAC

INIVAC, founded in 2020 to address digital needs arising from COVID-19, is a locally established service offering a range of digital solutions at affordable prices.

The company is led by a young, innovative founder whose mission is to empower micro-businesses through technology. The INIVAC team, composed of technology and business experts, prides itself on creativity and versatility. With deep global market knowledge, they provide simple yet effective digital services to help businesses achieve greater productivity and stability.
